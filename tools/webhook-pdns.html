DCDR

Quick facts

Role

Targets
PowerDNS at 160.30.63.133
Zone

What it is

Plugs into cert-manager so wildcard certs and per-host certs in *.apps.sub.comptech-lab.com can be issued from Let's Encrypt without exposing port 80 publicly.
